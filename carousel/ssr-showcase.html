2. Progressive Enhancement

Before JavaScript: <faceless-carousel> is an unknown element — children render as a visible block-level sequence. Search engines and users on slow connections see all content immediately. No shadow DOM, no hiding.

After JavaScript: The component upgrades, calls _init(), inserts prepend and append buffer clones into the light DOM, measures viewport dimensions, and starts the animation loop. From this point it behaves identically to a CSR carousel.
