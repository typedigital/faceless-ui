1b. Email: HTML Standard vs. pattern

The HTML5 standard accepts foo@bar as a valid email — no TLD required. The pattern attribute on <faceless-input> enforces stricter rules. Try both with foo@bar (no TLD) and foo@bar.com (with TLD).
